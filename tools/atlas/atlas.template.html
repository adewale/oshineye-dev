Adewale Oshineye Atlas

Atlas

An interactive map of the work rather than a list of it. Every repository is a star; its cluster is the theme it belongs to, the maroon lines trace lineage — what grew from what — its colour is the language, and its size is how many other projects descend from it. Drag a node, scroll to zoom, hover to light its neighbourhood, click to open it on GitHub. Filled stars are currently-featured projects.

Settling the map…
  • size = projects descended from it
  • line = lineage (parent → child)
  • cluster = shared theme
  • filled = currently featured
