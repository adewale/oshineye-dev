Why does the same text take different space?

At size 0.9, “Hello, outlines” is 6.02 units wide in Inter and 8.10 in JetBrains Mono. The same characters start from the same left edge at the same size. The rulers disagree because the fonts assign different spacing. Which letters account for most of the difference?

  2. Each glyph moves the pen on by its own advance

    Ticks: where each glyph leaves the pen. Boxed: Inter’s narrowest, l, and widest, H.

    A tick marks where the pen starts the next glyph; the distance between ticks is its advance. Width is the sum of the glyphs’ advances, and each font sets its own. JetBrains Mono gives all 15 characters 0.540, so its line is 15 × 0.540 = 8.10. Inter gives narrow letters less room. Each l gains 0.322 units in Mono, helping account for the opening’s wider line.

  4. To fill its wider cell, JetBrains Mono draws a different l

    will shape matching pair any Inter glyph with a JetBrains Mono one?

    The dashed boxes mark the l’s advances. JetBrains Mono gives it more room and draws a flag and a foot; Inter uses a simpler stem. The character is the same, but its path has changed. A shape matcher ignores position and size, so the outlines themselves must supply the correspondence.

    • l. Inter. advance 0.218. l. JetBrains Mono. advance 0.540.
  5. Answer: across the two fonts, none of the 14 glyphs pair

    Every glyph pairs with its 60% copy; with the other font, none do.

    TransformMatchingShapes compares outlines after removing position and size. The 60% copy preserves those paths, so its glyphs pair with the originals. In this phrase the other font changes the paths: none pairs across the fonts. A character name identifies a letter, not a particular outline.

Check yourself

  1. A space adds no outline. Why can adding one still move the next word farther right?

    Show the answer

    A space has an advance even though it draws no ink. The pen moves by that advance before the next glyph, so the line grows without another outline.

  2. Which letter of “Hello, outlines” gains the most width in JetBrains Mono, and how does the font fill the extra room?

    Show the answer

    The l: 0.218 in Inter, 0.540 in JetBrains Mono, three times over. JetBrains Mono draws its l with a flag on top and a foot turned right, so it is a different outline, not a stretched one.

  3. Could moving or uniformly resizing the Inter glyphs make them match the JetBrains Mono paths that failed to pair?

    Show the answer

    No. The matcher already removes position and uniform size before comparing paths. Those operations cannot remove differences in the outlines themselves.

  4. The label “Hello, outlines” is measured in Inter, then drawn in JetBrains Mono to fit that width. Is this label stretched or squeezed?

    Show the answer

    Squeezed: this phrase advances 8.10 units in Mono against 6.02 in Inter. The renderer presses it into the narrower recorded width. A different label needs its own measurements.
