A renewal rule written for 90-day certificates renews 45-day ones twice as often

The CA/Browser Forum’s Baseline Requirements cap how long a publicly trusted TLS certificate may be valid. The cap was 398 days until 15 March 2026 and 200 days from then. It falls to 100 days in March 2027 and to 47 in March 2029. When the lifetime shrinks, does a rule that renews 30 days before expiry still leave enough time between certificates? We will compare it with a rule tied to the certificate’s lifetime.
