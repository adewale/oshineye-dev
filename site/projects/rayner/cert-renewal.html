When should a short-lived certificate renew?

The CA/Browser Forum caps how long a public TLS certificate may last: 398 days until 15 March 2026, 200 from then, 100 from March 2027 and 47 from March 2029. A renewal margin that used to occupy a small part of the lifetime can now exceed the whole lifetime. What should a job do when its certificate lasts less than the configured margin?
