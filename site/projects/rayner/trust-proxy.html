Which address should Express call the client?

Requests are meant to reach Express through a CDN and then a load balancer, so every connection Express sees comes from the load balancer, 10.0.0.5. req.ip is meant to be the sender’s address instead. Setting trust proxy to 2 tells Express that two proxies stand in front of it; the path through the CDN has two. But the load balancer also accepts connections from the internet, so an attacker can skip the CDN. The addresses are examples.
