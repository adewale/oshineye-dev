Changing one meeting of a weekly series stores another VEVENT with the same UID

Alice’s calendar app saves her stand-up as one VEVENT. DTSTART is the first meeting, Monday 2 November 2026 at 09:00 UTC. RRULE repeats it weekly, and COUNT=6 stops after six meetings, with DTSTART counting as the first. The list shows the meetings a calendar computes from those two lines.

  1. A weekly stand-up is stored as one VEVENT

    DTSTART is the first meeting; RRULE with COUNT=6 makes six.

    Alice’s calendar app saves her stand-up as one VEVENT. DTSTART is the first meeting, Monday 2 November 2026 at 09:00 UTC. RRULE repeats it weekly, and COUNT=6 stops after six meetings, with DTSTART counting as the first. The list shows the meetings a calendar computes from those two lines.

  4. One UID: at most one master, any number of overrides

    At a line’s end, a bar is one, a circle zero, a crow’s foot many.

    The same storage as a schema. Marks at a line’s ends say how many: a bar is one, a circle zero, a crow’s foot many. RFC 4791 puts every VEVENT with one UID in one calendar object: the master, which it defines as the one without RECURRENCE-ID, and the overrides, which may be stored without it.

    • ER diagram: 5 entities, 5 relationships:
      • CALENDAR_OBJECT → MASTER: holds (each CALENDAR_OBJECT has zero or one MASTER, each MASTER has exactly one CALENDAR_OBJECT);
      • CALENDAR_OBJECT → OVERRIDE: holds (each CALENDAR_OBJECT has zero or more OVERRIDE, each OVERRIDE has exactly one CALENDAR_OBJECT);
      • MASTER → OVERRIDE: is overridden by (each MASTER has zero or more OVERRIDE, each OVERRIDE has zero or one MASTER);
      • MASTER → EXDATE: skips (each MASTER has zero or more EXDATE, each EXDATE has exactly one MASTER);
      • MASTER → RRULE: repeats by (each MASTER has zero or more RRULE, each RRULE has exactly one MASTER);
      • CALENDAR_OBJECT: UID (text);
      • MASTER: DTSTART (datetime);
      • OVERRIDE: RECURRENCE-ID (datetime), DTSTART (datetime);
      • RRULE: FREQ (text), COUNT (int);
      • EXDATE: excluded (datetime).

    The server stores Alice’s object as her app saved it: one master with one RRULE and no EXDATEs, and one override, all with UID standup. The notes count each part in her copy, and parts her copy lacks are dimmed. Her calendar shows six meetings.

  6. The server refuses a second master or a second RRULE

    It answers 415; RFC 5545 only says RRULE SHOULD NOT repeat.

    Baïkal, running sabre/dav, checks each object saved to it and answers 415 to two VEVENTs without RECURRENCE-ID or to two RRULEs in one VEVENT. RFC 5545 says only that RRULE SHOULD NOT repeat, so here sabre is stricter, and the RRULE line now allows one. Bob’s copy, with no master, is accepted.
