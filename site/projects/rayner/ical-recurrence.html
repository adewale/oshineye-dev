What does a guest’s calendar keep when one meeting of a series changes?

Alice’s calendar app saves her stand-up as one VEVENT. DTSTART is the first meeting, Monday 2 November 2026 at 09:00 UTC. RRULE repeats it weekly, and COUNT=6 stops after six meetings, with DTSTART counting as the first. The list shows the meetings a calendar computes from those two lines.

  4. One UID: at most one master, any number of overrides

    At a line’s end, a bar is one, a circle zero, a crow’s foot many.

    The storage rules explain why Bob can keep one occurrence without Alice’s master rule. Marks at a line’s ends say how many: a bar is one, a circle zero, a crow’s foot many. RFC 4791 puts every VEVENT with one UID in one calendar object: the master, which it defines as the one without RECURRENCE-ID, and the overrides, which may be stored without it, as Bob’s is.

      • EXDATE: excluded (datetime).
  5. Alice’s copy: two VEVENTs, one UID

    One master and one override; her calendar shows six meetings.
