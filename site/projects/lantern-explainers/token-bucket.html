A token bucket refills when a request arrives

The bucket starts full at five tokens, and each accepted request spends one. allow(now) adds refill for the time since the last call, so no background timer runs. The chart will plot the balance over time; the dashed line is the one token a request needs.

Refill per second: 1 token

  1. Five tokens of burst capacity

    The bucket starts full at five tokens, and each accepted request spends one. allow(now) adds refill for the time since the last call, so no background timer runs. The chart will plot the balance over time; the dashed line is the one token a request needs.

      • 7: return 200;

  2. Six at once: five 200s, then a 429

    Six requests arrive at time zero. No time has passed, so refill adds nothing and the line drops one token per request. The sixth finds 0.0 tokens, under the dashed line, and gets 429.

    • Requests R1 to R10 at their arrival times: 5 answered 200, R6 429, 4 waiting;

  3. Refill at 1/s: R7–R9 get 429, 200, 429

    Between calls the line climbs at 1 a second: (now − lastRefill) × 1. R7 at 0.5 s finds 0.5 and R8 at 1 s finds 1.0; R9 arrives at the same instant as R8 and finds what R8 left. R7 (0.5) and R9 (0.0) are under the dashed line, so they get 429.
