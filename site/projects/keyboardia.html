Keyboardia

Collaborative music app

Keyboardia is a browser-based multiplayer step sequencer for making polyrhythmic patterns together in real time.

Create beats together, then remix the session.

It combines instant session creation, Web Audio synthesis, sampled instruments, per-step automation, and WebSocket collaboration. The result is a small playable instrument that also behaves like a shareable web artifact.

What it does

  • Lets up to 10 players jam together in the same session.
  • Supports per-track step counts from 3 to 128 for polyrhythmic patterns.
  • Offers 64 sound generators: Web Audio synths, Tone.js FM/AM synths, and sampled instruments.
  • Adds parameter locks for per-step pitch, volume, and tied-note automation.
  • Synchronizes effects, swing, patterns, and session state across collaborators.
  • Creates share links, remixes, immutable published sessions, and QR-code handoffs.
